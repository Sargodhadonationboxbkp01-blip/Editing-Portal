    User accounts

    Create accounts for Office Zimedars, Supervisors, Auditors, District Zimedars and Box Zimedars. Each person signs in with their own Emp Code and password and only sees the screens for their role.

    Picture storage

    Delete old pictures to free Google Drive space. Only the picture files are deleted — every request, its details, replies and history stay in the portal.

    Delete pictures by date

    Choose the dates the requests were received. Pictures of requests received between these two dates (including both days) will be deleted.

    Quick choice:

    Deleted pictures go to the Google Drive Trash of the portal owner and can be restored from there for 30 days. Empty the Trash to free space immediately.

    Delete requests by period

    Removes whole requests (details, replies, history and pictures) received between two dates. This cannot be undone. To delete one request, open it from All records and use Delete this request. Tip: use Export on the All records tab first to keep a copy.

    Portal link

    Send this link to your team on WhatsApp or by SMS. Anyone who opens it sees the sign-in page and signs in with their own Emp Code and password. Nobody needs a file. On a phone they can tap Add to Home screen in the browser menu to keep it like an app.

    Phone alerts with sound (Telegram, free)

    The portal can only make a sound while it is open. For alerts that arrive even when the page is closed, create a free Telegram bot and paste its token here. Everyone then connects their own Telegram once (menu → Phone alerts).

    1. In Telegram open @BotFather → send /newbot → choose a name and a username ending in bot.
    2. BotFather replies with a token like 123456789:AAH.... Copy it.
    3. Paste it below and press Save bot.

    What Office Zimedars can see

    Show or hide the My report tab (their dashboard of times, late and replied requests). Switch it off to hide it, on to show it again. It takes effect for everyone within a minute.

    WhatsApp messages (free)

    Works right away, no setup. When someone sends, approves, returns or re-sends a request, a green Open WhatsApp button appears with the message already written for the right person. They only tap Send. This needs that person's WhatsApp number in their account.

    Optional: fully automatic messages. The free CallMeBot service can send the message by itself, with no tap. Each person who should receive automatic messages does this once:

    1. Follow the steps at callmebot.com (“Free API WhatsApp”): save the CallMeBot number in the phone and send it the activation message from that person's WhatsApp.
    2. CallMeBot replies with a short key.
    3. In the Users tab, edit that person's account and paste the key in “CallMeBot key”. Press Send test to check.

    CallMeBot is an independent free service and can be slow or limited at times. If an automatic message fails, the portal still shows the Open WhatsApp button, and nothing is lost.

    Storage

    Pictures take up Google Drive space. Use the Storage tab to delete old pictures (or whole old requests) by date. Only the picture files are removed; every request and its history stay.
